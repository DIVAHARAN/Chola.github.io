Selected projects

A selection of embedded and electronics work. Detailed files, schematics and performance data can be shared where project permissions allow.

PROJECT 01

STM32H745 Master Controller

4-layer controller integrating dual isolated CAN, 4G connectivity, flash, isolated RS-485 power monitoring, Ethernet PHY, USB and USB Type-C interfaces.

PROJECT 02

1.2 kW PMSM Motor Controller

STM32G0B1 controller with isolated bus-voltage and current sensing, CAN communication and closed-loop braking-resistor control. Prototype tested with a 400 V DC supply and load in a controlled lab setup.
